Intelligent Computational Imaging Lab

Grounded AI that sees, reasons, and acts.

ICL is rooted in computational imaging, where rigorous mathematics grounds every image in the physics of how it was measured. That grounding now carries into learning multimodal, multiscale representations and building and adapting foundation models that stay faithful to the world they describe.

We close the loop with agents that act in the physical world and team with people, treating every input, measurement and human guidance alike, as valuable yet uncertain.

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence, Seoul National University. Led by Se Young Chun.
